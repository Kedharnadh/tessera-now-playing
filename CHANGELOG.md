# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- The tile: the cover, title and artist of any Home Assistant media player, at every size from 1x1 to 4x4, with up to
  six keys, a progress bar and times when there is room.
- The card a tap opens: the cover large, the title and artist, a seek bar with its times, the transport and volume keys,
  a volume bar, and the lyric window with the line being sung lit.
- Optional lyrics through a helper sensor, mirroring Kedharnadh/ha-lyrics-card: LRCLIB's exact lookup, then its search,
  synced lines or plain lines paced by the song, and a small cache.
